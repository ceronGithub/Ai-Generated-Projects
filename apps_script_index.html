Help & Guide
Getting Started
📅
Step 1 — Select Date RangePick a From and To date. Use the same date in both fields to export a single day.
📬
Step 2 — Fetch EmailsClick Fetch Emails to search your Gmail. Large inboxes may take a moment — the progress bar shows status.
✏️
Review & EditUse the table to view, edit, or remove emails before exporting. Changes only affect the export — your Gmail is untouched.
💾
Step 3 — ExportChoose CSV, PDF, or Text and click Download. The file saves directly to your device — nothing goes to Google Drive.
Export Formats
📊
CSV / ExcelBest for analysis. Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet app. Includes all fields and a summary sheet.
📕
PDFOpens a print dialog — choose "Save as PDF" to download. Best for archiving or sharing.
📄
Text FilePlain .txt with one email per block. Best for reading in a text editor or scripting.
Privacy & Security
🔒
Runs inside your Google AccountMailVault is a Google Apps Script. It never contacts external servers — all processing happens inside Google's own infrastructure.
👁
Read-only Gmail accessMailVault only reads emails. It cannot send, delete, or modify anything in your Gmail.
Troubleshooting
⚠️
"Gmail access was revoked"You may have removed MailVault from your Google account permissions. Use the Reconnect button that appears, or reload the page.
🔍
No emails foundCheck your date range. Gmail search uses UTC dates, so emails near midnight may appear on a different day.
🐢
Fetch is slowLarge date ranges with thousands of emails take longer. Try narrowing the range to a week at a time.
💡
MailVault processes emails in batches of 500. For very large mailboxes, split into multiple smaller exports by date range.

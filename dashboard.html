How to Add a Product

1

Fill in the Product Name & Category

Enter the product name (required). For category, type the display name like Men, Women, or Unisex. The slug is the lowercase version used in URLs — e.g. men, women.

2

Set the Price

Enter the selling price (required). You can also enter an Original Price to show a crossed-out price and a sale badge on the product card.

3

Add a Product Image

Upload your image to imgbb.com (free, no sign-up needed) → copy the Direct link → paste it into the Image URL field.

4

Add Sizes & Colors

Enter sizes and colors separated by commas — e.g. XS,S,M,L,XL and Black,White,Grey. These will appear as selectable options on the product page.

5

Set Featured & Active

Featured — shows the product on the homepage. Active — makes it visible in the shop. Uncheck Active to hide without deleting.

6

Save & Done

Click Save Product. It saves to Firebase and auto-creates inventory rows for each size/color at 0 stock. Update stock from the Inventory tab.

💡

Free image tip: Use imgbb.com to host product images for free. Upload → click the image → copy the link ending in .jpg or .png.
